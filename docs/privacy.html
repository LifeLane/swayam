Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

1. Data Collection & Sovereignty

SWAYAM GPT is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. All text generation, document processing, and memory storage happen locally on your device by default.

2. Local Storage

Chat history, memories, and document embeddings are stored locally within the app's secure sandbox. We do not transmit this data to any servers.

3. Cloud Integrations (Opt-in)

If you explicitly configure an external AI provider (e.g., OpenAI, Gemini) or a cloud-based MCP service, the data you send to those specific models will be governed by their respective privacy policies. You have full control over when and if external networks are used.

4. Analytics and Telemetry

We do not collect crash reports, usage analytics, or telemetry data. Your app experience remains entirely private.
